Company Travel Guidelines
✈️ Flights
Book economy class for domestic flights under 6 hours. Business class requires manager pre-approval. Book at least 14 days in advance when possible. Use company-preferred carriers where available.
🏨 Hotels
Standard rate cap is $250/night (major metro) or $175/night (other). Book through approved platforms. Extended stays beyond 7 nights require VP sign-off. Incidentals charged to personal card.
🍽️ Meals & Incidentals
Per-diem allowance: $35/day domestic, $55/day international. Alcohol is non-reimbursable unless a client entertainment exception is approved. Keep all receipts for claims over $25.
🚗 Ground Transport
Use rideshare or public transit where practical. Rental cars require manager approval. Mileage reimbursed at current IRS rate. Parking receipts required for reimbursement.
📋 Expense Reporting
Submit expense reports within 30 days of travel. Attach receipts for all items over $25. Missing documentation may result in non-reimbursement. Use the Reimbursable Expenses tracker in the Travel tab to log costs on the go.
🔒 Approval Flow
All travel estimated over $500 requires manager approval before booking. Submit via the Approval tab. Managers must respond within 3 business days. Emergency travel: contact your manager directly.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you. Fides collects only the information required to provide its travel planning and booking assistance features. We do not sell, rent, share, or otherwise disclose your personal data to any third party, advertiser, or external organization.

What we collect: Your email address, display name, travel preferences, booking records, expense notes, and manager relationship are stored securely to personalize your experience. Approval requests are visible only to you and your designated manager.

AI interactions: Messages sent to the AI Travel Agent are processed via the configured AI provider (e.g., Google Vertex AI) solely to generate your response. Conversation history is session-only and is not stored permanently.

Third-party APIs: Flight and hotel search queries are forwarded to travel APIs (e.g., Duffel) to retrieve results. Only search parameters (locations, dates) are transmitted — never your personal account details.

Data deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting your organization's Fides administrator.

Terms of Service

Data you willingly provide to Fides

By using Fides, you acknowledge that you are voluntarily providing the following information to help Fides deliver its travel assistance services:

  • Account information — your email address and chosen display name, used to identify your account and personalize your experience.
  • Travel search data — origin, destination, and travel dates entered during trip searches, used solely to retrieve flight and hotel results on your behalf.
  • Booking selections — flights, hotels, and ground transport you select and submit for manager approval.
  • Expense notes — purchase descriptions and amounts you log in the Reimbursable Expenses tracker, stored for your personal reference and reimbursement submissions.
  • Preferences — your notification settings, preferred airline/hotel/transport, and dark mode choice.
  • AI chat messages — questions and prompts you send to the AI Travel Agent, forwarded to the AI provider to generate your response.
  • Feedback — any text you voluntarily submit via the feedback box, logged to help improve Fides.
  • Location (if enabled) — your approximate coordinates when you use "Use my location," used only to autofill your trip origin.

None of this data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. It is used exclusively to operate and improve Fides for you and your organization.
