Corporate travel guidelines
All travel must be booked in accordance with company policy. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Hotel stays must not exceed the per diem rate for the destination city. All expenses over $50 require a receipt. Reimbursements are processed within 10–15 business days of submission. Personal travel combined with business travel must be declared and approved in advance.
Data & privacy
Your location and travel data are used only to power Fides features and are never sold to third parties. You can delete your data at any time from the Account tab. We use industry-standard encryption for all sensitive information.
